How SonicSentinel Operates

Four synchronized phases deliver military-grade acoustic threat detection to commercial and civic infrastructure.

PHASE 01

Acoustic Ingestion

High-fidelity microphones continuously sample ambient audio at 44.1kHz into an encrypted circular ring buffer.

PHASE 02

Neural Extraction

Instant Fast-Fourier Transform (FFT) generates Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs) in volatile RAM.

PHASE 03

Dual-Model AI

Simultaneous inference evaluates waveforms across 10 mandatory threat classes with confidence score agreement.

PHASE 04

Automated Slew

Integrated PTZ cameras automatically swivel to the acoustic azimuth, triggering visual lock-on and police dispatch.
